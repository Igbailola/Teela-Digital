About Teela

GOOD DIGITAL PRODUCTS AREN'T JUST DESIGNED. THEY'RE UNDERSTOOD, TESTED AND ENGINEERED.

Teela began in 2026 as a design academy and mentorship community helping aspiring designers and developers learn how to build digital products.

Today, Teela brings that same design and engineering mindset to businesses, startups and brands looking to create, improve or establish their digital products and presence.

We combine design, research and technology to create digital experiences that are useful, purposeful and built for the real world.

Founded 2026
Approach Research Design Engineering
Based Lagos, Nigeria
Working Globally
