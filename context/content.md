# 20. Writing Rules

All website copy should be:

- Clear
- Concise
- Confident
- Human
- Intelligent
- Internationally understandable

Avoid:

- Corporate jargon
- Empty marketing claims
- Exaggerated promises
- Unsupported statistics
- Fake client claims
- Fake testimonials
- Fake awards
- Fake partnerships
- Fake case-study results

Never fabricate evidence of Teela's experience.

If information is unavailable, use a clear placeholder or omit it.

---

# 21. Copy Principle

Prefer:

> We design and build digital products.

Over:

> We leverage innovative technologies to deliver transformative digital solutions.

Prefer:

> We help businesses understand their users and improve their products.

Over:

> We provide cutting-edge customer-centric optimization solutions.

The writing should sound like a capable team speaking to another capable team.
